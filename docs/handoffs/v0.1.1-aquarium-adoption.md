# Haepari v0.1.1 adoption handoff

This is the producer-owned operating handoff for [TASK-045](../roadmap/EPIC-010.md#task-045), [HP-REQ-087](../requirements.md#hp-req-087) and [V72](../verification.md). The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) alone owns acceptance. The generic [producer packet template](haepari-to-aquarium.md) and the historical [TASK-028 packet](../records/TASK-028-producer-handoff.md) retain their original scope.

## Owner and release boundary

The Haepari producer prepares the candidate packet and explains compatibility and rehearsal limits. The operator coordinating **Aquarium v0.1.24 official adoption** owns the later official-combination verification, consumer coordination, host cutover and rollback decision. Aquarium owns any consumer source or configuration change and its acceptance.

Keep the existing shared installation explicitly selected at its current dev-named root, `~/.haepari-aquarium-dev/`, until the verified official Haepari v0.1.1 and Aquarium v0.1.24 combination is available and the host operation is authorized. Keep independent development at `~/.haepari-dev/`. Epic completion or Haepari publication does not trigger migration. The deleted `~/.haepari-release` history is not an input and must not be recreated.

The intended shared destination is the native default `~/.haepari/`, resolved to the owning user's absolute private path. A directory name does not establish service family or ownership. Preserve the same release installation, instance and label; do not seed another controller by copying its database.

Keep original reports, raw runtime identities and evidence locators in that private packet, under the producer's retained custody through the final audit and any agreed consumer handoff. ## Official artifact and combination gates

Before host adoption, the responsible operator must:

1. Obtain the official Haepari v0.1.1 archive from its release authority, verify its archive/executable/paired-skill inventory and source correspondence, and bind it separately from the unpublished candidate packet. Any changed payload invalidates affected candidate observations until renewed verification establishes applicability.
2. Obtain the official Aquarium v0.1.24 payload and its source/manifest/skill identities. Apply Aquarium's own final-distribution and installed release-acceptance rules to the exact Haepari/Aquarium combination, including its native Mulgae/grant and required Podway inputs. Producer compatibility alone cannot replace that acceptance.
3. Record actual platform/session/Automation/UTM/SSH prerequisites, original failures and unobserved limits. An old permission toggle, package build or preparation document is not a current controller-identity permission observation.
4. Obtain authority for the exact host service/consumer changes and backup custody. Publication, installation, configuration changes and the migration operation retain separate authority.

## Release-time cutover and rollback

Follow the complete [native relocation procedure](../operations.md#v011-home-separation-and-relocation) and [home/relocation contract](../contracts/home-and-relocation.md). The operational owner performs these stages only after the gates above:

1. Suspend participating consumers and drain Jobs, leases, advisory requests/grants and recovery/cleanup holds through their owners. Capture installation/history/evidence, selected resources and current external-reference baselines. Historical FAIL/INCOMPLETE remains history; an unresolved hold remains a blocker.
2. Remove only the exact saved source service. Verify native process cessation, original lock freedom, unloaded label and absent exact plist. Socket unreachability does not prove drain or termination.
3. Keep both immutable executables outside both moving roots and the backup tree. Save the fixed official target release-v2 plan with matching instance, owner, label/domain/plist and limits. Keep the selected 2/1 global/host limits and existing `macos-main` and `mulgae-review` identities; do not silently recreate them.
4. Prepare an absent private same-filesystem destination and a unique backup directory beneath `~/.haepari-backups/`, outside both roots. The plan generates the relocation ID; record that ID together with the chosen backup path rather than editing the returned plan to rename either. Save the target binary's exact relocation-plan `data` object. Apply and reconcile only that plan; do not renew its identity after an uncertain response. Require verified backup and complete receipt before separately installing the target service.
5. Verify the same installation, retained history/evidence and resources/limits at the target. Before admitting a verification Job or new grant, record that new work crosses the old plan's rollback boundary; do not promise reverse-move recovery after that point. Run the explicitly authorized bounded local Job and native advisory acquisition/return, checking actual cleanup and evidence. A later failure requires supported reconciliation of the new state. Inventory and then change only approved live consumer/MCP selections; preserve parent/borrowed-grant semantics. Resume consumers only after the complete verification.
6. Retain original plans, executables, journal/history, backup and actual operation report under the named owner. Backup disposal is a separate decision.

**Rollback gate.** Before consumer resumption or new work, stop both service identities and use the same plan's native rollback only if its exact original identity and logical-history guards still pass. A startup can advance native clock/audit metadata; do not assume rollback remains legal after activation. Never restore a copied backup over newer work, downgrade a schema, run an unsupported old executable on upgraded state, or turn a historical path into rerun authority. Use the supported v0.1.1 binary at the restored root. After a settled reverse, a later forward move requires a new predecessor-bound plan; stale mutations refuse.

A present unbound backup stays unproven and untouched. Unknown publication siblings are not adopted or swept. On refusal, preserve the original evidence and journal and return to the owning recovery procedure. Neither this runbook nor its prepared packet authorizes shared host migration, a message, consumer acceptance or release.
