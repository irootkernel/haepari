# TASK-012 managed core freeze decision

Decision date: 2026-10-03. Effective when TASK-012 becomes Accepted in the [canonical roadmap](../roadmap.md). This record defines the candidate F1 boundary; it does not independently accept the Task or Epic. The accepted Task commit and its review/verification evidence identify the exact frozen bytes.

## Scope and authorities

F1 freezes implemented managed Job, local resource, result, run-event and envelope schemas; lifecycle/workload/evidence/cleanup/verdict and retry enums; stable errors and operation exits; admission canonicalization; list/event/log/artifact cursors; machine-readable help; native local runner receipts and the implemented advisory lock contract. Semantic owners remain [CLI/jobs](../contracts/cli-and-jobs.md), [resource/runner](../contracts/resource-and-runner.md), [managed transitions](../contracts/managed-transitions.md), [artifacts](../contracts/artifact-and-profile.md) and [locks](../contracts/locks.md). The accepted code's typed schemas/validators and those owners form the complete field inventory; machine help is an implemented command synopsis, not an assertion that every planned design command is available.

Production remains a private Unix controller with the local auto-Job backend. Frozen support does not qualify actual UTM/SSH, guest runner/VM identity fields (F2), manual completion and consumer integration conventions (F3), optional consumer environment locks and installation designs, configurable retention, performance or release services. Local execution is not a sandbox and ancestry discovery retains the TASK-004 best-effort limitation. These boundaries cannot be erased by a fake backend or passing build.

## TASK-012 field and compatibility decisions

- `haepari.result/v1` gains optional `logs` inventories and `evidence_gaps` (`path`, `reason`, `offset`, `dropped_bytes`). Existing `artifacts`, protected paths, native outcomes, recovery observations and phase timings remain immutable. Log and missing-output gaps prevent complete evidence; supplementary recovery never upgrades a terminal verdict.
- Native `haepari.runner-request/v1` adds optional `log_limit_bytes`; omitted/zero means 256 MiB combined stdout/stderr. `haepari.runner-exit/v1` adds optional `log` prefix manifest, limit, dropped bytes and gap. Old exact receipts without either field preserve their canonical digest and remain readable without fabricated log facts. All emitted non-optional native receipt fields, including zero-valued `Slot`, booleans and identity members, must be present with their JSON types; duplicate, unknown, alias and null scalar keys reject. Existing native `LocalIdentity` member names remain capitalized to preserve exact receipts. Shared schema/binding/PATH owners remove coordinated copies without changing their identities.
- New `haepari.artifact-manifest/v1`, `haepari.artifact-page/v1`, `haepari.purge-intent/v1`, `haepari.purge-receipt/v1`, `haepari.evidence-recovery-intent/v1`, `haepari.evidence-recovery-receipt/v1` and `haepari.help/v1` describe delivery and separate retention effects. Manifests retain original result and separate supplementary outcomes; page tokens are temporary installation/Job/file incarnation fences. Export strips them. Error/exit vocabulary does not change.
- New advertised capabilities are `artifact-delivery/v1` and `evidence-retention/v1`, joining `managed-execution/v1`, `durable-observation/v1`, `advisory-lock/v1` and `managed-recovery/v1`. CLI and hello share one owner with independently pinned consumer expectations. Backend qualification remains separate.
- Collection defaults are 1000 declared outputs and 1 GiB aggregate with 64 KiB buffers. Logs retain a 256 MiB prefix and drain beyond it. Delivery pages are 64 KiB; manifests cap at 3000 result files plus one log, supplementary inspection at 1000 resource-scoped operations, and each explicit purge attempt at one fixed minute, including evidence-fence waiting and chunked sealed-input validation. No automatic expiration or GC is introduced.
- Purge atomically journals exact owned incarnations and unavailable admission before effects. It deletes eligible owned file/event payloads and unshared sealed object bytes. Permanent admission/cancellation/Job recovery replay, cursor ownership/floor and small private original result/run/phase/retention authority remain. Event deletion reads the permanent ownership cursor, independent of the public tombstone projection, and retains small recovery intent/phase compensation bindings below the public floor. Exact recovery replay still returns its original receipt; changed token/evidence conflicts after purge and reopen. Public resource recovery snapshots retain the unavailable status and resolved-evidence projection without exposing that original result. Retry preserves the saved original expected revision. Unknown or replaced paths, missing positive-identity roots and object-less ledger fixtures refuse.
- Protected-evidence recovery records original result/path/owner/output facts and admitted fixed budgets. Disposal names evidence loss and permanent request authority before effects. Recollection commits and re-verifies supplementary inventory before cleanup under its saved cleanup deadline and a tracked durable worker; expiry cannot renew per-file budgets or authorize source deletion. Live or unproven owners block recovery; restart does not renew deadlines. A complete exact `resolved` projection can satisfy retention, while separate observed health must lift quarantine. Unsupported production VM plans remain tokenless inspection.
- Admission uses canonical typed closed JSON, lowercase SHA-256 and normalized POSIX relative paths; map keys sort and array order remains significant. Events use globally monotonic IDs with permanent Job ownership; owner validation precedes unavailable-result reporting. Prefix deletion retains floors/resync semantics. Lists use scoped opaque continuation without a multi-page snapshot promise. Logs use file identity/exact offsets and explicit gaps. Artifacts use exact offsets plus signed incarnation token and complete export hash verification.

The retention correction preserves these existing F1 promises without a wire or storage schema change. Initial TASK-012 acceptance remains the freeze decision; a corrective implementation does not introduce a new capability or weaken the frozen contract.

## EPIC-009 approved F1 delta

TASK-036 records Master's D1–D3 approval before implementation: job/result v2 opt into absence-aware settlement, while v1 admissions and terminal history retain their original semantics. Phase receipts and native runner formats remain v1 because their original facts and shapes are preserved; partial evidence with verified cleanup is selected only by v2 policy. Separate recovery authority receives a v2 schema when its semantics change. This decision is permission to implement and test, not TASK-036 acceptance or real-VM qualification.

The same TASK-036 delta adds the new immutable local-workspace/v1 binding before v2 workload delivery. Original phase/native records retain v1 shapes; the new binding preserves the prepared inode through recovery and pruning.

V2 live reconciliation adds separate reconciliation-required/v2 and availability-restoration/v2 events. Only verified terminal settlement may restore the exact availability revision owned by that Job's observation failure. Original operator restrictions, changed revisions and legacy failures retain their prior authority.

Failed recovery/v2 observations may add bounded, deduplicated diagnostic events to an available retained finished Job without changing its revision, original result, capacity, resource availability or recovery authority. Purged admissions receive no new diagnostics. Ordinary diagnostic recording retains its terminal guard; successful exchanges emit no failure event.

TASK-036 supplementary absence uses separate evidence-recovery-intent/v2 and evidence-recovery-receipt/v2 private payloads. Original workspace/attestation authority and observation times bind absent required paths; verified files and absent paths are unique and disjoint. V1 payloads reject the new absence fields and retain their original policy. Public supplementary v1 projections stay frozen. Exact native completed-cleanup recovery authority is retained through pruning; late host completion keeps failed cleanup timing and INCOMPLETE while permitting separate evidence resolution and resource health after every original file is verified or covered by approved v2 positive absence. Native workload facts and historical terminal results remain immutable.
